Which Metal Roofing Fits Your Building?

Homes in established neighborhoods: 5V crimp keeps a traditional character, while standing seam gives a cleaner profile. Both are common choices for a primary residence.

New construction: Builders on the growing edges of Lake City often spec standing seam for its concealed fasteners and contemporary lines.

Commercial and roadside buildings: Small offices, storage units and service buildings near the interstate exits often use PBR for coverage and value. Your installer will confirm what the building and code require.

Rural property: Out toward Fort White, Olustee or Five Points, owners frequently match the house to a barn or equipment shed with the same color.

Questions Worth Asking Before You Sign

A good installer will gladly answer: Which metal roofing profile do you recommend and why? Will you tear off the old roof or go over it? What underlayment and fasteners meet code at my address? Will you pull the permit with the City of Lake City or Columbia County? How are vents, chimneys and valleys flashed? Keep the answers in your written quote.

Picking a Color

Light colors like white, ivory and light gray are favorites for staying cooler in a Lake City summer. Bronze, green and gray tones blend with tree-lined streets and wooded lots, and darker shades suit modern exteriors. Ask your installer for physical samples to compare outdoors.
